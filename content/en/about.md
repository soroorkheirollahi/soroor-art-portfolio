Soroor Kheirollahi is a visual artist and painter based in Mahshahr, Khuzestan, Iran. With a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Painting from Islamic Azad University, her work is deeply rooted in Surrealism, exploring the boundaries between subconscious psychological landscapes and physical reality.

Beyond her independent studio practice, Soroor is passionate about art education. She has been teaching painting and sculpture at Kanoon (Institute for the Intellectual Development of Children and Young Adults) since 2016, where she currently serves as the Center Manager. Her dedication to nurturing young talents goes hand in hand with her own creative journey.

Through her paintings and sculptures, Soroor invites viewers to reflect on emotional introspection and the transformative nature of human perception.
