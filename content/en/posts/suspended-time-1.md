This painting is a window into the human soul, exploring the weight of sadness and the passage of time. It speaks of moments when life feels heavy and shadowed, yet we quietly try to hold onto a spark of life inside. The work captures a deep, emotional stillness—a reflection on our vulnerabilities, hidden griefs, and the fragile hope we carry through the passing days.
