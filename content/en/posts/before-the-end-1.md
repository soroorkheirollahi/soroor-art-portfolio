This work reflects on the passage of time and the feeling of being left behind in a difficult moment—a moment when something is coming to an end, yet the possibility of continuing remains. Loneliness and distance meet the steady movement of time, creating a space where sorrow and hope exist side by side. The work reminds us that even in the darkest moments, we can still wait for a small change.
