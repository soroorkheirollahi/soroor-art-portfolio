I created this work at a time when the news of war and threat had once again returned to our daily lives — July 2026. My dominant feeling in those days was a paradox: on one side, the heaviness and bleakness of wartime, and on the other, an intense will to live and to continue. As a woman from southern Iran, it is my personal experience that even in the darkest conditions, life persists, stubbornly renewing itself. In my work I often explore the relationship between women, nature, and the body in the face of violence. This painting is the outcome of that vision: growing, right in the shadow of the bomb.
