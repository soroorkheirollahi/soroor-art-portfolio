In this painting, the aged background of an old map and lost geography serves as a terrain for a woman suspended between the invisible borders of crisis and isolation. The pink hair and the manipulated face—featuring a button for an eye and stitched lips—reflect a fractured self-awareness and a painful attempt to control inner suffering. It is as if the figure herself takes up needle and thread to forcefully mend her shattered fragments. The button replacing an eye and the sealed lips speak of a deliberate refusal to witness surrounding catastrophes and the suppression of a voice amidst collective turmoil. This artwork is an exploration of unhealed wounds, haunted geographies, and human resilience where survivaldemands the dissection and stitching of one’s own emotions.
