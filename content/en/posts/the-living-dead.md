I created this work from a sense of exhaustion and a need to pause—a moment in which one closes one’s eyes and seeks refuge within, amid the turmoil outside. The experience of living in the shadow of war and insecurity remains in the emotional background of the painting. Yet, for me, the work is not only about suffering; it is also about searching for a moment of calm and holding on to life. In my paintings, I bring personal experience and collective memory into a surreal space, allowing me to express feelings that are difficult to articulate directly.
