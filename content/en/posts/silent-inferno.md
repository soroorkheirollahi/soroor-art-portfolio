This work grew out of a feeling of pressure and imposed silence that many of us — especially women — experience in everyday life; a condition in which one must appear “alive” even when breathing is hard. It takes me back to a period of my own life when the inner struggle to preserve calm and identity was my deepest concern. My approach in painting is to narrate human experience through surreal signs — not a direct depiction of reality, but a mental space for what cannot be said. This painting is a narrative of resilience: beauty and life, right in the midst of constriction and constraint.
