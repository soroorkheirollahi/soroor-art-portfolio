\## 🎨 Artist Statement

My artistic practice is grounded in Surrealism, exploring the boundary between subconscious psychological landscapes and physical reality. Through deliberate compositions, symbolic imagery, and nuanced color palettes, my paintings investigate emotional introspection and the transformative nature of human perception.
